Privacy notice

Eve (Hermes) is a private app with a single user, its owner. It accesses only the owner's own Google account, and only to carry out tasks he asks for.

Data is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
